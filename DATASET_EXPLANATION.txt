A. SHIP FUEL & EMISSIONS DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File Location:
  data/ship/ship_fuel_efficiency.csv

Overview:
  Contains monthly operational, routing, fuel consumption, efficiency, and CO2
  emission profiles for marine vessels operating along commercial maritime
  shipping corridors in the Gulf of Guinea / Nigerian coastal waters.

Record Count:
  1,441 records (header + 1,441 data rows)

Columns & Schema:
  1. ship_id             : Vessel identifier (e.g., NG001, NG002, NG003, ...)
  2. ship_type           : Category of marine vessel
                           - Oil Service Boat
                           - Fishing Trawler
                           - Surfer Boat
                           - Tanker Ship
  3. route_id            : Maritime transit route
                           - Warri - Bonny
                           - Port Harcourt - Lagos
                           - Lagos - Apapa
                           - Escravos - Lagos
  4. month               : Operating month (January through December)
  5. distance            : Travel distance for the voyage (in nautical miles / km)
  6. fuel_type           : Marine fuel used (HFO = Heavy Fuel Oil, Diesel = Marine Diesel)
  7. fuel_consumption    : Total fuel consumed during the voyage
  8. CO2_emissions       : Total carbon dioxide emitted during the transit (kg CO2)
  9. weather_conditions  : Sea state / ambient weather encountered (Calm, Moderate, Stormy)
 10. engine_efficiency   : Percentage efficiency rating of the propulsion engine (e.g. 70% - 95%)

Role in Maritime Grid Simulation:
  - Establishes base load curves and dynamic power requirements for vessel propulsion.
  - Enables modeling of hybrid vessel powertrains (replacing or augmenting diesel/HFO
    with battery banks and solar arrays).
  - Supplies carbon baseline for calculating emission reductions when utilizing clean
    energy or port microgrid electrification.

B. BATTERY AGING & DEGRADATION DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Folder Location:
  data/battery/

Overview:
  Originates from the NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE) Li-ion
  battery aging dataset. Comprises commercial 18650-sized Li-ion cells subjected
  to repeated charge, discharge, and Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS)
  tests under controlled ambient temperatures (4°C, 24°C, 43°C/44°C) until reaching
  End-of-Life (EOL, 30% fade in rated capacity).

Structure:
  1. metadata.csv:
     - Master lookup table indexing every charge, discharge, and impedance cycle.
     - 7,566 total test cycles across battery cells (B0005 through B0056).
     - Columns:
       * type                : Cycle type ('charge', 'discharge', 'impedance')
       * start_time          : Timestamp vector [Year, Month, Day, Hour, Min, Sec]
       * ambient_temperature : Operating temperature (4, 24, 43, or 44 °C)
       * battery_id          : Cell identifier (e.g., B0005, B0006, B0047, etc.)
       * test_id             : Test index sequence for that specific battery
       * uid                 : Unique global test sequence identifier
       * filename            : Corresponding detailed time-series CSV file in data/
       * Capacity            : Measured discharge capacity (Ahr) (discharge cycles only)
       * Re                  : Electrolyte resistance (Ohms) (impedance cycles only)
       * Rct                 : Charge transfer resistance (Ohms) (impedance cycles only)

  2. data/ (6,209 CSV files: 00001.csv to 06191.csv):
     - Raw high-frequency sampling for individual operational cycles:
       * Charge cycle files:
         - Voltage_measured (V)
         - Current_measured (A)
         - Temperature_measured (°C)
         - Current_charge (A)
         - Voltage_charge (V)
         - Time (seconds elapsed)
       * Discharge cycle files:
         - Voltage_measured (V)
         - Current_measured (A)
         - Temperature_measured (°C)
         - Current_charge / load (A)
         - Voltage_charge / load (V)
         - Time (seconds elapsed)
         - Capacity (Ahr)
       * Impedance cycle files:
         - Sense_current (complex number)
         - Battery_current (complex number)
         - Current_ratio (complex number)
         - Battery_impedance (complex frequency response, Ohms)
         - Rectified_Impedance (calibrated impedance, Ohms)

  3. extra_infos/ (9 README files):
     - Technical specification and test protocols for each batch of cells:
       * README_05_06_07_18.txt   : Cells 5, 6, 7, 18 (24°C room temp, CC-CV charge, 2A discharge)
       * README_25_26_27_28.txt   : Cells 25-28 (24°C, 0.05Hz square-wave pulsed discharge)
       * README_29_30_31_32.txt   : Cells 29-32 (43°C elevated temp, 4A discharge)
       * README_33_34_36.txt      : Cells 33, 34, 36 (24°C, 4A and 2A discharge to 20% fade)
       * README_38_39_40.txt      : Cells 38, 39, 40 (Variable temps 24°C & 44°C, 1A-4A loads)
       * README_41_42_43_44.txt   : Cells 41-44 (4°C low temp, 4A & 1A loads)
       * README_45_46_47_48.txt   : Cells 45-48 (4°C low temp, 1A constant load)
       * README_49_50_51_52.txt   : Cells 49-52 (4°C low temp, 2A constant load)
       * README_53_54_55_56.txt   : Cells 53-56 (4°C low temp, 2A constant load)

Role in Maritime Grid Simulation:
  - Models Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) performance and State-of-Charge (SOC).
  - Models battery thermal dynamics and capacity degradation (State-of-Health, SOH).
  - Simulates dynamic battery charging from solar PV and onboard diesel generators,
    as well as high-draw discharge during vessel maneuvering and peak load conditions.

C. ENVIRONMENTAL, SOLAR & METEOROLOGICAL DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File Location:
  POWER_Point_Daily_20200101_20241224_012d72S_002d89E_LST.csv
  (Currently in project root)

Overview:
  Derived from NASA's POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources) database.
  Provides daily temporal resolution meteorological and solar irradiance data
  spanning 5 full years (January 1, 2020 through December 24, 2024; 1,821 daily rows)
  for latitude -12.7248 S, longitude 2.8868 E (South Atlantic / West African maritime zone).

Variables Present:
  1. Date Identifiers:
     - YEAR, MO, DY : Calendar year, month, and day.

  2. Atmospheric & Weather Variables:
     - PS             : Surface Pressure (kPa)
     - WD10M, WD50M   : Wind Direction at 10 meters and 50 meters (degrees)
     - WS10M          : Wind Speed at 10 meters (m/s)
     - WS10M_MIN      : Minimum Wind Speed at 10 meters (m/s)
     - WS10M_MAX      : Maximum Wind Speed at 10 meters (m/s)
     - WS10M_RANGE    : Daily Wind Speed range at 10 meters (m/s)
     - WS50M_MIN      : Minimum Wind Speed at 50 meters (m/s)
     - WS50M_MAX      : Maximum Wind Speed at 50 meters (m/s)
     - WS50M_RANGE    : Daily Wind Speed range at 50 meters (m/s)
     - WS2M           : Wind Speed at 2 meters (m/s)
     - RH2M           : Relative Humidity at 2 meters (%)
     - T2M_MIN        : Minimum Temperature at 2 meters (°C)

  3. Solar Radiation & Irradiance Variables:
     - ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN  : All-Sky Surface Shortwave Downward Irradiance (kW-hr/m^2/day)
     - CLRSKY_SFC_SW_DWN  : Clear-Sky Surface Shortwave Downward Irradiance (kW-hr/m^2/day)
     - ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DNI  : All-Sky Surface Shortwave Direct Normal Irradiance (kW-hr/m^2/day)
     - ALLSKY_SFC_LW_DWN  : All-Sky Surface Longwave Downward Irradiance (kW-hr/m^2/day)
     - CLRSKY_SFC_PAR_TOT : Clear-Sky Surface Total Photosynthetically Active Radiation (kW-hr/m^2/day)
     - ALLSKY_SFC_PAR_TOT : All-Sky Surface Total Photosynthetically Active Radiation (kW-hr/m^2/day)

Role in Maritime Grid Simulation:
  - Solar Irradiance: Drives the Photovoltaic (PV) power generation curves for vessel-mounted
    or floating/port solar installations.
  - Wind Speed & Direction: Supports maritime aerodynamic drag calculations, wave/weather
    impact on vessel transit resistance, and potential offshore wind turbine generation.
  - Temperature & Humidity: Influences solar PV panel efficiency (derating factors) and
    ambient cooling/heating performance of BESS battery enclosures.
